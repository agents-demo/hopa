- Open Settings to change Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Display or Battery.
- Wi-Fi: Settings > WLAN. Toggle the switch.
- Bluetooth: Settings > Bluetooth. Toggle the switch.
- Display: Settings > Display & brightness.
