# Uber Eats

- Open https://www.ubereats.com in the built-in browser.
- Read the page and use the visible text on links and buttons; never invent coordinates.
- Signing in and the delivery address are handed to the user.
- **Checkout is a payment-classified action: always ask before placing the order.**
  Show the restaurant, items, fees and total when the page shows them.

## Common flow

1. Choose a delivery address.
2. Search a restaurant or a dish.
3. Add items to the cart.
4. Review the total, then ask before placing the order.

## Limits

- Never place an order or change a tip without approval.
- Prices and fees can change between screens; read the final total, not an earlier one.
