# LinkedIn

- Open https://www.linkedin.com in the built-in browser.
- Read the page and use the visible text on links and buttons; never invent coordinates.
- Signing in is handed to the user. HOPA never types a password or a one-time code.
- Do not post, message, connect or apply without approval.

## Common screens

- Feed: posts and a "Start a post" button.
- Search: the search field at the top.
- Profile: name, headline, experience.
- Jobs: search, filters, "Easy Apply" (approval required before submitting).

## Limits

- LinkedIn blocks some automated access. If the page shows a challenge, stop and
  hand over; never solve a CAPTCHA.
