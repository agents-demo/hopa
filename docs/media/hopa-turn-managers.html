HOPA · architecture in two pictures

One turn, and the loop inside it

A person asks for something, HOPA checks whether it may act, then repeats one small question until the job is done: what is the next move? The answer is always an action, a question to the person, or the final answer.

1. The whole turn

Say this: “A turn is a short doorway, then one loop.”

  • Plan first is optional; when it is on, nothing runs before a person approves.
  • Before the loop, HOPA opens the app the goal needs, if any.
  • The loop is one box here: everything that repeats lives in the next picture.

2. The loop

Say this: “One question at a time, and only three kinds of answer.”

  • An action changes the screen; the loop reads it again.
  • A question goes to the person; the answer brings the loop back to the start.
  • A final answer ends the turn; nothing else is touched.
